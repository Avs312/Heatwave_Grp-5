Inside the Heatwave Intelligence Infrastructure

Published by System Operational Command | Last Updated: August 2026

1. System Objective

The primary objective of HIS is to supply real-time climate telemetry and predictive thermal risk assessment across vulnerable regions. By processing hourly microclimate metrics, the platform helps municipal authorities issue timely public warnings, deploy emergency cooling infrastructure, and minimize heat-induced clinical emergencies.

"Extreme thermal events are no longer standard seasonal spikes—they represent severe public health hazards requiring active operational readiness. The Heatwave Intelligence System bridges raw meteorological telemetry directly with emergency action response teams."

— Dr. A. Sharma, Chief Meteorologist & Disaster Management Lead

2. Telemetry & Data Sources

Calculations are computed using continuous ambient multi-tier sensor feeds and historical metrics compiled from the following streams:

  • AWS Sensors: IoT weather stations providing local temperature, atmospheric pressure, and humidity metrics.
  • IMD Database: Climatological baselines and regional trend models managed by the Indian Meteorological Department.
  • Satellite Feeds: Geostationary thermal imagery (INSAT-3D satellites) measuring Land Surface Temperature (LST) indices.

3. Forecast & Alert Workflow

Systematic operational steps triggered during heat event evaluation:

  1. Data Acquisition: Acquire data from IMD historical data and AWS observations.
  2. Data Analytics: Data preprocessing and storage along with Spatio-Temporal analysis.
  3. Risk Modeling: Forecasting, early prediction, and severity classification.
  4. Validation: Forecast validation and local heat assessment using AWS observation.
  5. Decision Support: Dashboard, visualization, and LLM-based advisories.
  6. Alert Generation: Broadcast operational warnings to public safety leads and automated alert systems.

4. Stakeholders & Role-Based Actions

The system maintains direct operational integrations with key emergency partners (NDMA, healthcare units, transit networks, and agricultural boards). Role permissions and available action protocols include:

  • System Administrators
    • Configure sensor ingestion rates and alert thresholds.
    • Manage system authentication and API permissions.
  • Meteorological Analysts
    • Verify machine-generated thermal anomalies and perform manual reviews.
    • Issue advisory bulletins to municipal authorities.
  • Emergency Responders & Municipal Authorities
    • Deploy mobile cooling shelters and public hydration stations.
    • Coordinate EMS dispatch teams based on thermal risk maps.
  • General Public & Media
    • Receive real-time public emergency alerts.
    • Access local heat intensity maps and cooling shelter locations.

System Overview

The HIS is a multi-layered climate monitoring and predictive analytics platform designed to provide actionable insights during extreme heat events. It integrates real-time telemetry, historical climate data, and predictive modeling to support public safety and emergency response.

Abbreviations & Glossary

AWS
Automatic Weather Station: Automated meteorological sensor arrays that log ambient environmental data in real time.
IMD
Indian Meteorological Department: The primary government body responsible for weather forecasting and climate data governance.
HW
Heatwave: A prolonged period of unusually high thermal stress exceeding pre-established regional thresholds.
SHW
Severe Heatwave: Critical heat levels significantly above baseline limits that pose severe threats to public health and infrastructure.
